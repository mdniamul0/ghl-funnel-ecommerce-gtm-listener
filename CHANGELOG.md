# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (2026-11-07)

- First release of the free GoHighLevel funnel GA4 ecommerce listener.
- `view_item` on the sales page, `begin_checkout` on the order page, `purchase` on the thank-you page.
- Edit the `FUNNEL` paths at the top for your own funnel.
- Price read from the page, closest to words like "today" or "total", so crossed-out prices are skipped.
- No $0 purchases, and no second purchase when the thank-you page is reloaded.
